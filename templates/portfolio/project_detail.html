GaadiMandi

India's Next-Gen Dealership & Automotive Network

A full-stack automotive marketplace and dealership operating system that gives independent dealers their own branded digital showroom, live inventory, financial telemetry, and direct buyer communication.

Role
Product Designer + Full-Stack Developer
Type
Automotive Marketplace + B2B SaaS
Focus
Digital Showrooms • Inventory • Operations

The Problem

Dealers Lack Digital Ownership

Independent dealerships often depend on third-party listing portals instead of having a professional website of their own.

Inventory is Hard to Manage

Dealers need to understand active capital, vehicle aging, refurbishment costs, holding-cost bleed, and actual profit.

Buyers Need More Trust

Used-vehicle buyers need inspection information, verified documents, transparent pricing, and easy direct communication with dealers.

One Platform. Two Experiences.

GaadiMandi connects a dealer's public digital showroom with a private dealership operating system — keeping inventory, leads, finances, and customer-facing listings connected in real time.

Dealer Website Live Inventory Buyer Inquiry Command Center Sale + Profit

Instead of sending customers screenshots or scattered marketplace listings, a dealer can share one personalized GaadiMandi URL containing their live inventory.

Customers see available vehicles, pricing, inspection scores, verification status, and WhatsApp contact — all from the dealer's own branded showroom.

TourCraze

Intelligent Travel Planning & Collaborative Group Expense Platform

A full-stack travel platform that connects algorithmic trip scheduling with multi-party financial collaboration. Built with Django and client-side telemetry, it replaces disjointed spreadsheets and split-bill calculators with an integrated system for discovering destinations, generating day-by-day itineraries, and settling group expenses.

Role
Product Designer + Full-Stack Engineer
Type
Travel SaaS + Collaborative Platform
Focus
Procedural Scheduling • Debt Algorithms • Financial Telemetry

The Problem

Fragmented Planning

Travelers juggle separate apps for research, itinerary scheduling, stay bookings, and route pacing, leading to disjointed plans.

Manual Assembly

Structuring realistic day-by-day travel timelines with appropriate pacing, cost estimates, and geographical grouping requires tedious manual curation.

Group Expense Friction

Splitting trip costs across uneven contributions, shared receipts, and multiple members creates complex debt webs that are difficult to reconcile.

The Core Engineering

Procedural Itinerary Synthesis & Greedy Debt Simplification

Unlike conventional travel directories that display static catalogs, TourCraze generates structured multi-day schedules dynamically. The itinerary engine synthesizes target duration, traveler count, budget constraints, and travel styles into structured daily timelines with cost estimates and transit pacing.

For group finances, TripSplit implements a Greedy Minimum Cash Flow settlement algorithm. By computing the net balance of every traveler and repeatedly pairing the largest net debtor with the largest net creditor, it collapses tangled multi-party debt webs into the minimum number of direct settlement transactions.

TradeLab

AI-Powered Trading Education & Multi-Market Analysis Platform

An all-in-one web-based educational and market analysis platform designed to simplify technical analysis and market literacy for retail market participants. It unites an 8-milestone progressive trading curriculum, real-time multi-market charting hubs (Crypto, Forex, and Indian Equities), computer vision chart pattern recognition (YOLOv5), a production-ready Pine Script v5 algorithmic indicator suite, and live financial RSS news feeds into a single dark fintech command center.

Role
Product Designer + Full-Stack Developer
Type
Fintech Portal + Applied AI Web App
Focus
Technical Analysis • Computer Vision • Multi-Market

The Problem

Indicator Noise & Clutter

Most public TradingView indicators overwhelm charts with excessive visual clutter, lagging repaints, and conflicting signals rather than providing clean, actionable trend structure.

Tool & Market Fragmentation

Retail traders are forced to juggle disparate websites for live charting, session timing clocks, economic news feeds, and indicator scripts, creating a disjointed workflow.

Unstructured Learning & Complexity

Beginners face a steep learning curve identifying complex chart formations and risk management rules without a structured, step-by-step curriculum.

One Platform. Every Market.

TradeLab brings structured education, live multi-asset charting, and computer vision pattern detection together into a focused trader command center designed around five continuous stages:

Learn Analyze Understand Practice Trade Smarter

Platform Subsystems

8-Milestone Learning Roadmap

Step-by-step curriculum with interactive lesson modules, strategy blueprints, diagrams, and Investopedia references.

Crypto Trading Hub

11 live TradingView pairs (BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, etc.) with liquidation heatmap tools and real-time market data.

Forex & Commodities Hub

Live charts for major currency pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY) plus Gold, Crude Oil, US30, and NASDAQ.

Indian Market Hub

Dynamic charts for NIFTY 50, BANK NIFTY, SENSEX, and top blue-chip equities (Reliance, HDFC Bank, TCS, Infosys).

AI Chart Pattern Recognition

Custom-trained YOLOv5 model pipeline with 20 pattern classes, drag-and-drop uploader, and pan/zoom preview.

Pine Script Indicator Center

Copy-paste ready Pine Script v5 algorithmic code with Triple EMA ribbons, ATR trailing stops, and candlestick counters.

Live Financial News Feed

Asynchronous XML/RSS news ingestion via Python `feedparser` streaming real-time global macroeconomic headlines.

Global Market Session Clocks

Comparative session breakdowns for New York, London, Tokyo, and Mumbai with volatility window indicators.

The 8-Milestone Structured Curriculum

STAGE 01 — FOUNDATIONS
Market Mechanics

Order types, bid/ask spreads, market participants, and multi-asset class mechanics.

STAGE 02 — TECHNICALS
Structure & Trends

Support and resistance levels, trendlines, and Higher-High / Lower-Low price action structure.

STAGE 03 — INDICATORS
Momentum & Volatility

EMA ribbons, RSI divergences, MACD crossovers, and Bollinger Band volatility breakouts.

STAGE 04 — PATTERNS
Chart Formations

Head & Shoulders, Double Tops/Bottoms, Triangles, Wedges, and Flag breakout strategies.

STAGE 05 — RISK MGMT
Position Sizing & R:R

1:2+ Risk-to-Reward rules, stop-loss calculations, portfolio drawdown protection, and capital preservation.

STAGE 06 — PSYCHOLOGY
Emotional Discipline

FOMO mitigation, revenge trading avoidance, and maintaining disciplined execution consistency.

Includes Milestones 7 & 8 covering Algorithmic Pine Script Backtesting and Live Execution Governance.

What Makes TradeLab Different

An educational workspace built like an institutional analytical terminal.

TradeLab bridges theory and practice: instead of reading static PDF guides, learners immediately cross-reference concepts against live TradingView charts, test automated Pine Script algorithms, and scan setups with computer vision AI.

✨ 100% Educational & Objective Designed strictly as an educational and technical analysis research tool with zero real-money trading risks or gamified gambling schemes.

Built With

Django powers the modular routing, multi-asset views, and server-side RSS feeds, while PyTorch / YOLOv5 provides the computer vision model architecture for chart pattern bounding. Embedded TradingView widgets deliver real-time multi-market data without expensive third-party data subscriptions.
